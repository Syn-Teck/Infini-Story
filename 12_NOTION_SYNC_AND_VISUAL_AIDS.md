# Notion Sync and Visual Aids Map

## GitHub-to-Notion mapping

| Canonical change | Notion view |
|---|---|
| current scene, actors, established time | État de campagne & Canon Lock |
| character resources, equipment, condition | relevant character page |
| ownership, ammunition, healing, currency | Inventaire & ressources |
| NPC/faction/relationship state | Compagnons & relations; Monde, lieux & factions |
| quest, revelation or knowledge label | Quêtes & lore |
| ledger or rules checkpoint | Journal & règles |

## Visual-aid policy

- Quick sketch: mood, discovery or memorable moment.
- Tactical board: only when relative positions are established and matter.
- Location recap: revealed portions only.
- Never reveal unexplored areas, hidden enemies, traps, treasure, identities or lore.
- Label each image with its scene/checkpoint when relevant; written GitHub canon wins over the image.
